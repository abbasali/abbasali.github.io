LaraconUS 2025 was absolutely wonderful and I learned and enjoyed every moment of it! The conference exceeded all my expectations with its incredible lineup of speakers and the vibrant Laravel community atmosphere. I was particularly excited to learn about Laravel Boost, a revolutionary package that accelerates AI-assisted development by providing the essential context and structure that AI needs to generate high-quality, Laravel-specific code. The announcement of the new Forge UI was another highlight, bringing significant improvements to the server management experience and making deployment workflows even more streamlined for Laravel applications.
